Before You Leave… One Little Promise? 🥹🌷

“Namma friendship-la sandai varalaam, misunderstandings varalaam, life-la evlo changes vandhaalum… namma oruthara oruthar marandhuda koodaadhu di. 💗”

Our Promise to Keep 🤝

Whatever happens in life, let's promise to never leave one another alone when things get difficult.

Sandai vandhaalum pesi solve pannuvom. Misunderstanding vandhaalum oruthara oruthar purinjukka try pannuvom.

Yaarukkavadhu kashtam vandhaa, ‘naan irukken di’ nu solla oru friend-aavadhu pakkathula iruppom.

Happy moments-la mattum illa, difficult situations-layum orutharukku oruthar support-ah iruppom.

Namma ellaarum perfect-ah irukkanum nu avasiyam illa. Aana namma friendship-ku care, honesty, respect, and understanding kuduppom.

Life namma ellaaraiyum different paths-la kondu ponaalum, namma friendship-ai value panna marakka maattom.

No matter what life brings, let's choose kindness, keep communicating, and never stop caring for one another.

Always us. Always a little world of our own. Sisters for life. 🌸💗

Promise accepted! 🥹💗

Indha promise-a vida mukkiyam namma orutharukku oruthar kaattura care dhaan. Whatever happens, let's keep choosing friendship. 🌷
